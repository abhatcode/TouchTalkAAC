What is AAC?

Communication is a human right

Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC) tools help people who cannot speak — or have difficulty speaking — to express themselves. TouchTalk replaces expensive dedicated AAC devices with a free app that works on any phone, tablet, or computer. Tap a symbol, build a sentence, press Speak. It's that simple.

FAQ

Common questions

Does TouchTalk collect any data about me or the user?
No. TouchTalk collects absolutely zero data. There are no analytics, no tracking pixels, no server calls. Everything — your word banks, settings, and custom phrases — is stored only on your device using the browser's local storage. Nothing ever leaves the device.
Does it work without internet?
Yes. Once you've visited the app once, it installs a service worker that caches the entire app. After that, TouchTalk works completely offline — including text-to-speech, which uses your device's built-in speech engine, not the internet.
What languages are supported?
Text-to-speech supports every language your device supports (typically 30–60 languages on iOS and Android). The app interface is currently in English. You can add words and phrases in any language — the TTS engine will attempt to speak them correctly.
Is this a medical device?
No. TouchTalk is a communication aid, not a medical device. It has not been certified by the FDA, CE, or any other regulatory body. It is not a replacement for professional AAC assessment or therapy. For complex communication needs, please consult a speech-language pathologist.
Can I add my own words and pictures?
Yes. Open the app, go to Customize, and you can add new categories, symbols with custom photos or emojis, and quick phrases. Everything is saved to your device and persists between sessions.
Does it work with switch access or eye-tracking?
TouchTalk is designed with accessibility in mind. All interactive elements are keyboard-focusable, which makes it compatible with most switch access systems. Full eye-tracking support is on the roadmap for a future version.
Why is it free?
Communication is a human right. Dedicated AAC devices can cost thousands of dollars, putting them out of reach for many families. TouchTalk exists to change that. It is maintained as a free, open project with no commercial interests.
